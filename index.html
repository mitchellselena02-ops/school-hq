How your data works

School HQ is designed to be useful at $0 per month.

Stored locally: courses, assignments, study resources, and preferences are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a school account or third-party server by this file.

Not included yet: account sign-in, cross-device cloud sync, school LMS integration, and push notifications. Those need additional setup.
